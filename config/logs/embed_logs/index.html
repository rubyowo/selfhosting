Embed logs

Emoji Mention​

All emoji used within these logs is from The Hangar server, however, due to Zeppelin logs using webhooks to send messages, you will be able to access these emoji even if your bot user is not on the server, so long as your bot has the Use External Emojis permission.

If you would like your bot to have access to the emoji on the server, join us and look at the #add-your-bot channel!

Name in config: logs​

  # [...]
